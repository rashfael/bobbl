# Glossary / Glossar / Glossario

Trilingual terminology reference for ice cream and gelato formulation. **Primary abbreviations follow Italian convention** (as used by De Giglio and the broader European gelato tradition).

## Core Balance Parameters

| Italiano | Deutsch | English | Abbr. | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Potere Dolcificante | Suesskraft | Sweetening Power | **POD** | Relative sweetness of a sugar compared to sucrose (= 100). Determines perceived sweetness of the mix. |
| Potere Anti-Congelante | Gefrierpunktabsenkung | Anti-Freezing Power | **PAC** | Relative freezing point depression of a sugar compared to sucrose (= 100). Determines hardness/softness at serving temperature. |
| Solidi del Latte Non Grassi | Magermilchtrockenmasse | Milk Solids Non-Fat | **SLNG** | Non-fat dry matter from milk: protein + lactose + minerals. Also known as MSNF in English literature. |
| Solidi Totali | Trockensubstanz | Total Solids | **TS** | Everything in the mix that isn't water. Sum of fats, sugars, SLNG, stabilizers, and other dry matter. |
| Equivalente Destrosio | Dextrose-Aequivalent | Dextrose Equivalent | **DE** | Measure of reducing sugar content relative to dextrose (= 100). Classifies glucose syrups and maltodextrins. Higher DE = shorter sugar chains = sweeter and more anti-freeze effect. |

## Composition & Structure

| Italiano | Deutsch | English | Abbr. | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grassi | Fett | Fat | — | Total fat content (%). Primarily from cream, butter, egg yolk, nuts, or chocolate. |
| Proteine | Eiweiss / Protein | Protein | — | Total protein content (%). Mainly from SLNG (casein, whey), egg, or nuts. Critical for emulsification and air cell stability. |
| Lattosio | Laktose | Lactose | — | Milk sugar naturally present in dairy. Contributes to POD (POD ~16) and PAC (PAC ~100). Excess causes sandy texture (crystallization above ~9% of water weight). |
| Aria / Overrun | Aufschlag | Overrun | — | Air incorporated during churning, expressed as % volume increase. Gelato: 25-40%, ice cream: 50-100%. |
| Zuccheri | Zucker | Sugars | — | Total sugar content from all sweeteners. Includes sucrose, dextrose, fructose, glucose syrups, etc. |
| Acqua | Wasser | Water | — | Water content of the mix (= 100% - TS%). Determines amount of ice crystal formation. |

## Sugar Classification

| Italiano | Deutsch | English | Abbr. | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Saccarosio | Saccharose / Haushaltszucker | Sucrose | — | Table sugar. Reference standard: POD = 100, PAC = 100, MW = 342.3 g/mol. |
| Destrosio | Dextrose / Traubenzucker | Dextrose (D-Glucose) | — | Simple sugar (monosaccharide). POD ~70, PAC ~190. Less sweet but much stronger anti-freeze than sucrose. |
| Fruttosio | Fructose / Fruchtzucker | Fructose | — | Fruit sugar (monosaccharide). POD ~170, PAC ~190. Very sweet with strong anti-freeze. |
| Zucchero invertito | Invertzucker | Invert Sugar | — | Equimolar mix of glucose + fructose from sucrose hydrolysis. POD ~95, PAC ~190. Prevents crystallization, hygroscopic. |
| Sciroppo di glucosio | Glukosesirup | Glucose Syrup | — | Partially hydrolyzed starch. Classified by DE value. Low DE (30-40) = mild sweetness, body builder. High DE (80-97) = approaches dextrose properties. |
| Maltodestrina | Maltodextrin | Maltodextrin | — | Hydrolyzed starch with DE < 20. Minimal sweetness (POD ~6-17), low PAC. Used as a body builder and solids booster. |
| Trealosio | Trehalose | Trehalose | — | Disaccharide (MW = 342.3). POD ~50, PAC ~100. Half as sweet as sucrose, same anti-freeze. Stabilizes proteins during freezing. |
| Inulina | Inulin | Inulin | — | Polysaccharide from chicory root. POD ~0-10, very low PAC. Functions as fat replacer and body builder, not technically a sugar. |

## Measurement & Analysis

| Italiano | Deutsch | English | Abbr. | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gradi Brix | Grad Brix | Degrees Brix | **Brix** | Percentage of soluble solids (mostly sugars) in a solution, measured by refractometer. 1 Brix = 1 g sucrose per 100 g solution. |
| Rifrattometro | Refraktometer | Refractometer | — | Optical instrument measuring refractive index to determine Brix. Requires temperature correction if not at 20 C. |
| Acidita titolabile | Titrierbare Saeure | Titratable Acidity | **TA** | Total acid content of fruit, measured by titration with NaOH. Expressed as % of dominant acid (citric for citrus, malic for stone fruits). |
| Bilancio | Bilanzierung | Balancing | — | The process of adjusting ingredient quantities to bring all parameters (POD, PAC, TS, fat, SLNG) into their target ranges simultaneously. |

## Ice Cream Types (German Classification)

| Italiano | Deutsch | English | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gelato alla crema | Cremeeis | Cream ice / Custard | Made with egg yolk and milk/cream. Min. 50% milk, min. 270 g whole egg or 90 g yolk per litre milk. |
| Gelato al latte | Milcheis | Milk ice cream | Milk-based, typically 70%+ milk. Lower fat than Cremeeis. |
| Gelato alla panna | Rahmeis | Cream ice cream | Min. 60% cream (Sahne). Rich, high-fat. |
| Sorbetto | Fruchtsorbet | Fruit sorbet | Dairy-free, fruit-based. Water + sugar + fruit. |
| Gelato alla frutta | Fruchteis | Fruit ice cream | Contains dairy + fruit. Min. fruit content varies by fruit type. |
| Ghiacciolo | Wassereis | Water ice / Ice pop | Max 3% fat, min 12% dry matter. |

## Stabilizers & Emulsifiers

| Italiano | Deutsch | English | E-Nr. | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Farina di semi di carrube | Johannisbrotkernmehl | Locust Bean Gum (LBG) | E410 | Galactomannan from carob seeds. Strongest hydrocolloid for ice crystal control. Requires hot hydration (~85 C). |
| Farina di semi di guar | Guarkernmehl | Guar Gum | E412 | Galactomannan from guar beans. Hydrates at lower temperatures than LBG. Good synergy with LBG. |
| Gomma xantana | Xanthan | Xanthan Gum | E415 | Fermented polysaccharide. Cold-soluble, pseudoplastic (shear-thinning). Synergistic with LBG. |
| Gomma arabica | Gummi arabicum | Gum Arabic | E414 | Tree sap exudate. Emulsifier and stabilizer. Lower viscosity than other gums. Good for sorbets. |
| Carragenina | Carrageen | Carrageenan | E407 | Sulfated polysaccharide from red seaweed. Prevents whey separation. Gels above 0.05%. |
| Gomma di tara | Tara-Gummi | Tara Gum | E417 | Galactomannan similar to LBG. Needs hot hydration for full function. |
| CMC | CMC (Carboxymethylcellulose) | Carboxymethyl Cellulose | E466 | Cellulose derivative. Cold-soluble. Must be dry-mixed with sugar before dissolving. |
| Lecitina | Lecithin | Lecithin | E322 | Phospholipid emulsifier from soy or sunflower. Natural alternative to mono-/diglycerides. |
| Mono- e digliceridi | Mono-/Diglyceride | Mono- & Diglycerides | E471 | Most common ice cream emulsifier. Promotes partial fat coalescence for smoother texture. |
| Fibra di agrumi | Zitrusfaser / Citrusfaser | Citrus Fiber | — | Dietary fiber from citrus peel. Water binder and stabilizer. Clean-label alternative. |
| — | Inulin | Inulin | — | Chicory root fiber. Fat replacer, body builder. See Sugar Classification above. |

## De Giglio Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Eiskoerper** | "Ice body" — De Giglio's concept that each main ingredient (fruit, nut, chocolate, etc.) requires its own individually balanced formula rather than a universal base. |
| **Bilancio del gelato** | The balance sheet / calculation of a gelato recipe, tracking all parameters (TS, POD, PAC, fat, SLNG, sugars) across every ingredient. |
| **Corpo** | "Body" — the structural quality of gelato determined by solids content, protein, and stabilizers. |
